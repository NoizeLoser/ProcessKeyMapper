

# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Principles

The ProcessKeyMapper community is built around collaboration, technical discussion, and mutual respect.

Our community brings together people with different backgrounds, opinions, experience levels, and approaches to software development.

We believe that different perspectives can help improve a project when they are expressed constructively.

Everyone participating in the ProcessKeyMapper community is expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

## 2. Expected Behavior

All community members are expected to:

- Treat other participants with respect and courtesy.
- Communicate in a constructive and professional manner.
- Respect different opinions and technical approaches.
- Provide useful arguments when disagreeing with a proposal.
- Keep discussions focused on ProcessKeyMapper and relevant topics.
- Accept reasonable criticism of their own ideas and contributions.
- Respect the project's technical direction and contribution procedures.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for both beginners and experienced developers.

Disagreements are allowed. Personal attacks are not.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats.
- Personal insults, humiliation, or targeted hostility.
- Discrimination based on personal characteristics.
- Deliberate provocation or repeated attempts to disrupt discussions.
- Spam, advertising, or unrelated promotional content.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Impersonating the Original Author or other community members.
- Misrepresenting a fork as the official ProcessKeyMapper project.
- Deliberately spreading false information about contributors or the project.
- Repeatedly ignoring moderation decisions or contribution requirements.

Technical criticism is permitted, including strong disagreement with a proposed implementation, provided it remains focused on the work rather than attacking the person.

## 4. Communication Guidelines

### 4.1 Issues

Issues should be used for bug reports, technical problems, and feature proposals.

Participants should provide relevant information and avoid turning technical reports into personal arguments.

### 4.2 Discussions

GitHub Discussions are intended for community conversations, questions, localization presentations, and exchanging ideas.

Participants should respect the topic of each discussion and avoid deliberately derailing conversations.

### 4.3 Pull Requests

Pull Requests are technical proposals, not personal competitions.

Reviewers and contributors should discuss implementation details objectively and provide clear reasoning for requested changes.

The Original Author retains the final decision regarding the acceptance of contributions, as described in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 5. Respect for Project Ownership

ProcessKeyMapper is maintained and controlled by its Original Author.

Community participation does not grant ownership of the project or authority over the Official Repository.

Participants must respect:

- The project's license.
- The contribution process.
- The repository's access restrictions.
- The Original Author's final decisions regarding project development.
- The distinction between the Official Project and independently maintained forks.

Disagreement with a project decision is allowed. Harassment, threats, or disruptive behavior in response to that decision are not.

## 6. Moderation

The Original Author is the primary maintainer and moderator of the official ProcessKeyMapper community spaces.

The Original Author may take moderation action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, actions may include:

1. A friendly reminder of the community rules.
2. A formal warning.
3. Removal of inappropriate comments or content.
4. Closing or locking disruptive Issues and Discussions.
5. Rejecting or closing contributions that violate community rules.
6. Restricting or blocking participation where platform moderation tools permit it.

The Original Author may consider the context, severity, repetition, and impact of the behavior when deciding how to respond.

Serious violations may result in immediate moderation action without a prior warning.

## 7. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the Original Author through the contact method published in the Official Repository.

When reporting an incident, provide:

- A description of what happened.
- Links to relevant Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, or comments.
- Relevant screenshots or other evidence, if available.

Reports should be made in good faith.

The Original Author will review reports and determine whether moderation action is appropriate.

## 8. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in official ProcessKeyMapper community spaces, including:

- The Official GitHub Repository.
- GitHub Issues.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Pull Requests.
- Other official communication channels operated by the Original Author for the project.

It also applies when someone is publicly representing the ProcessKeyMapper project or its official community.

## 9. No Guarantee of Participation

Participation in the ProcessKeyMapper community is voluntary.

The Original Author reserves the right to moderate official community spaces and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

This Code of Conduct does not guarantee anyone a right to have their Issue, Discussion, Pull Request, or proposal accepted.

## 10. Final Statement

The purpose of this Code of Conduct is not to prevent disagreement or criticism.

Its purpose is to ensure that discussions remain useful, respectful, and focused on improving ProcessKeyMapper.

**Different opinions are welcome. Disrespect is not.**
